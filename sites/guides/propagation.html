Workspace, Hygiene & Permissions

Set up a clean propagation station

  • Tray or baking sheet to contain mess
  • Sharp knife or secateurs, rubbing alcohol, kitchen roll
  • Labels + pencil, small pots or cell inserts, clear bag or dome
  • 50:50 compost : perlite (or 60:30:10 compost : bark fibre : perlite)

Sterilise between plants: wipe tools with alcohol between plants to reduce rot and fungal spread.

Ethics & permissions

  • Propagate only from plants you own or have permission to take from
  • Check Plant Breeders’ Rights before distributing
  • Never collect from the wild without consent

Softwood Cuttings Spring–Early Summer

  1. Take 8–10 cm tip below node, strip lower leaves.
  2. Insert into airy mix. Optional rooting hormone.
  3. Water & cover with bag or dome.
  4. Bright light, 18–22 °C. Vent daily.

Semi-ripe & Hardwood

  1. Semi-ripe: 8–12 cm, firm stems, airy mix.
  2. Hardwood: pencil-thick sticks, 2 nodes buried.
  3. Cool, ventilated spot over winter.

Avoid cold + wet compost.

Division

  1. Water plant day before.
  2. Split root ball into sections with shoots.
  3. Pot into fresh mix, trim top growth.

Layering

  1. Nick underside of stem at node.
  2. Pin to compost, keep moist.
  3. Cut free after rooting.

Water Propagation

  1. 10 cm cutting below node, remove lower leaves.
  2. Jar of water, change every 2–3 days.
  3. Pot at 3–5 cm roots.

Grafting (Advanced)

  1. Match scion/rootstock thickness.
  2. Cut 45°, align cambium, clip.
  3. High humidity 5–7 days.

Blackening stems

  • Too wet/cold. Refresh cut & mix.
  • Warm base, add airflow.

Wilt & crisp

  • Heat/sun stress. Shade lightly.
  • Reduce leaf area.

No roots

  • Material too old or cold.
  • Take fresh cuttings.

Stalled after potting

  • Too big pot or too rich mix.
  • Step up gradually.

FAQs

Water vs soil?
Water is fast for herbs; soil gives stronger roots for woody plants.
Can I root flowering stems?
Remove buds; focus energy on roots.
Heat mats needed?
Not essential but boosts semi-ripe cuttings.
First pot size?
Start small (7–9 cm). Avoid waterlogging.
