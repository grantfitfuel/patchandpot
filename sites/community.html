Guidelines & Safety

Be kind, be clear, be safe. Share experience, not judgement. Assume good intent.
Disclaimer: Patch & Pot provides a community space only. We do not vet items or users, and we do not handle payments or delivery. Swaps and meet-ups are at your own risk.
  • Privacy: Don’t post full addresses or phone numbers. Use public meeting spots.
  • Safety: Bring a friend for swaps. Meet in daylight in a public place.
  • Seeds & plants: Label variety and year. Avoid sharing diseased material.
  • Images: Only upload your own photos.
  • Reporting: Use “Report” under any post to flag concerns.
