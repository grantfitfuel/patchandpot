Soft Light — Winter (Variant A)

[00:00] Find a comfortable posture. Let your shoulders soften. If it helps, lower your gaze or gently close your eyes.
[00:08] Notice pale windows; let that small detail anchor you. Jaw unclenches, tongue rests easy.
[00:15] Breath pattern (first minute): Inhale for 4 · Hold for 2 · Exhale for 6. If this feels too long, shorten it without strain.
[00:20] ⟶ Inhale 1…2…3…4 · Hold 1…2 · Exhale 1…2…3…4…5…6
[00:35] ⟶ Inhale 1…2…3…4 · Hold 1…2 · Exhale 1…2…3…4…5…6
[00:50] Let your breath flow low into the belly; imagine it travelling to the base of your ribs. Notice kettle and steam.
[01:00] Second minute pattern: Inhale for 4 · Exhale for 8. No hold. Let the out-breath lengthen and soften.
[01:05] ⟶ Inhale 1…2…3…4 · Exhale 1…2…3…4…5…6…7…8
[01:20] ⟶ Inhale 1…2…3…4 · Exhale 1…2…3…4…5…6…7…8
[01:35] If thoughts wander, that’s okay. Return kindly to counting or to a small detail in front of you.
[01:50] Let go of the count. Notice how you feel. Shoulders, jaw, face—softer now.
[02:00] Gently open your eyes if they were closed. Name one thing you can appreciate in this moment.

Alternate: simply breathe a little deeper on the inhale and longer on the exhale, without counting. Comfort comes first.
